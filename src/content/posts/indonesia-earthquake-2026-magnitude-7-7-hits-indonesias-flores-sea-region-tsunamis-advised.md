# Indonesia Earthquake 2026: Magnitude 7.7 Strikes Flores Sea Region

## Introduction to the August 15, 2026 Flores Sea Earthquake

On August 15, 2026, early that morning, a powerful earthquake of magnitude 7.7 hit the Flores Sea area of [Indonesia](https://britannica.com). The severe tremors caused widespread shockwaves to be felt throughout East Nusa Tenggara and the adjacent islands. 

The earthquake took place at a shallow depth of about 10 to 15 kilometres. It was located roughly 68 kilometres northwest of Ende and caused immediate panic as people rushed from their buildings onto the streets. 

Following the initial shock, the relevant authorities quickly issued urgent tsunami warnings for coastal areas. This resulted in extensive international monitoring and searches by concerned observers around the world, such as those in the United States.

## Key Facts and Impact

* **Location of the Epicentre:** Offshore, about 68 km northwest of Ende, Indonesia, at a shallow depth of around 10 to 15 kilometres.
* **Magnitude:** 7.7 on the Richter scale, with a strong 6.1-magnitude aftershock occurring shortly afterwards.
* **Tsunami Situation:** [Tsunami Warnings](https://tsunami.gov) were immediately sent out by the regional disaster mitigation agencies, instructing people to evacuate to higher ground, before being officially withdrawn a few hours later.
* **Casualties and Damage:** Early reports indicate that there were at least two deaths, together with structural cracks in local houses, community buildings, and regional medical facilities.
* **Regional Vulnerability:** Indonesia lies directly on the active seismic [Pacific Ring of Fire](https://usgs.gov) and is therefore very prone to major tectonic shifts and frequent oceanic quakes.

## Emergency Response and Safety Advice

Local search and rescue teams were sent out right after the main shock to inspect the remote coastal villages and to assess the damaged infrastructure. Currently, emergency management services are still keeping an eye on the seismic equipment for any remaining aftershocks. 

People in the affected areas have been told to be careful about any housing that is structurally unstable. They are urged to adhere to the guidelines provided by the [Indonesian Agency for Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics (BMKG)](https://bmkg.go.id). 

Meanwhile, the global tracking service known as the [USGS Earthquake Hazards Program](https://usgs.gov) is still giving out live updates on the seismic activity in the region.
